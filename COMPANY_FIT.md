# PulseHype — Company and Team Fit

Matches are potential skills and employer fit, not evidence of company use, endorsement or partnership.

## Project thesis

A dashboard that collects stories from Hacker News and selected RSS feeds, then sorts them using simple engagement and keyword signals.

## Databricks

**Relevant public technical areas:** Data pipelines, ranking and analytics.

**Potential roles:** Data Engineer, Backend Engineer, Applied Scientist, Product Engineer or ML Engineer.

**Evidence to build:** Flask backend and static browser frontend. Sources include Hacker News and configured RSS feeds. Ranking uses a project-defined engagement-velocity heuristic and keyword-based sentiment/hype signals; it is not a validated measure of news importance or public opinion.
